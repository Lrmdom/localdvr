# Product Guidelines

## Prose Style
- Keep language clear, concise, and professional.
- Use technical terms accurately and avoid unnecessary filler.
- Focus on the *why* rather than just the *what*.

## Branding
- The project is a robust, modular Python service.
- Branding should reflect stability, efficiency, and local-first operation.

## UX Principles
- Focus on low resource consumption on local hardware.
- Prioritize resilience and self-recovery in all processes.
- Ensure clear and structured logging for maintainability.
